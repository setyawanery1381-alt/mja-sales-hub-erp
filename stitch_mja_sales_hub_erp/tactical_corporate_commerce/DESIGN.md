## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance enterprise interface engineered for mission-critical e-commerce operations, financial reconciliation, and inventory throughput. The design language marries executive-tier corporate gravitas with tactical utilitarian clarity—drawing direct inspiration from modern engineering suites like Stripe, Shopify, and Linear.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
Crafted for multi-channel operators, supply chain managers, financial controllers, and warehouse leads who demand zero-latency cognition. The interface conveys uncompromising precision, fiscal control, and uninterrupted operational momentum. Users experience a state of calm authority: high-density data remains legibly structured, financial health metrics are unmistakable, and offline synchronization states instill absolute data integrity.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Card-Based Tactical Modularity:** Dense, information-rich viewports composed of sharply delineated visual containers.
- **Low-Chrome Utilitarianism:** Chrome recedes; data leads. Structure is defined through precise hairline dividers and muted structural fills rather than heavy graphical decoration.
- **Fiscal Clarity & Status Primacy:** Semantic colors serve functional telemetry only—distinguishing cash flow surpluses, platform overheads, and sync pipelines at a glance.

## Colors

The palette employs a high-contrast corporate foundation paired with strictly regulated telemetry accents. Neutral tones preserve legibility in intense data environments, while semantic tones deliver immediate status awareness.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Anchor (`#0F172A` - Slate 900):** Represents structural authority. Deployed across navigation bars, primary command buttons, interactive active states, and top-tier headers.
- **Secondary Surface (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** High-clarity foreground container surface for analytical cards, modular tables, modal dialogs, and form inputs.
- **Canvas Base (`#F8FAFC` - Slate 50):** Cool ambient backdrop mitigating ocular strain during multi-hour data auditing and order routing.
- **Profit / Accent (`#10B981` - Emerald 500):** Denotes positive yield, net profit margin ratios, completed payment settlements, successful telemetry events, and spreadsheet sync confirmations.
- **Queue / Review (`#F59E0B` - Amber 500):** Designates pending logistics actions, ready-to-ship (RTS) triage, batch holding patterns, and background sync queues.
- **Overhead / Alert (`#EF4444` - Crimson 500):** Isolates operational friction, platform commissions, acquisition costs (ROAS deficits), stock depletion alerts, and return rejections.

### Text & Structural Neutrals
- **Text Dominant (`#0F172A`):** For mission-critical labels, numeric amounts, and headline hierarchy.
- **Text Subdued (`#64748B` - Slate 500):** For metadata, tabular column definitions, and secondary timestamps.
- **Border / Structural Divider (`#E2E8F0` - Slate 200):** Precise hairline separator used across panels, input strokes, and table rows.

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes a disciplined dual-font architecture engineered for rapid legibility and dense financial accounting.

### Font Pairings & Application
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Display & Structural Hierarchy):** Selected for its structured geometric forms and executive presence. Powers analytical dashboard cards, module titles, metric panels, and operational modal headers.
- **Inter (Functional Body, Forms, & Data Grids):** Selected for its neutral aesthetic and industry-leading performance in high-density data sheets. All numeric representations, SKU listings, SKU barcodes, currency calculations, and timestamps must render with font feature settings enabled: `tnum` (tabular numbers) and `cv02`, `cv03`, `cv04` for unambiguous glyph distinction.

### Layout Hierarchy Guidelines
- Metrics, revenue tallies, and inventory stock indicators must utilize `metric-display` with tabular numbers enabled to eliminate visual jitter across streaming updates.
- Section tags, badge labels, and table column heads must use `label-sm` in deliberate uppercase tracking when contextualizing tabular metadata.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a rigid, fluid 12-column analytical workspace designed to balance deep data density with clean visual organization.

### Viewport Adaptation & Grid Models
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12-column layout with fixed collapsable utility rail (260px), multi-stage dashboard view, `gutter-lg` (32px), and `margin-lg` (32px).
- **Tablet / Workstation Laptop (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with icon-condensed sidebar (72px), `gutter-md` (24px), and `margin-md` (24px). Analytical charts collapse into two-row modules.
- **Mobile / Tactical Handheld (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout with drawer-driven navigation, single-column cascading card streams, `gutter` (16px), and `margin` (16px). High-density tables reflow into swipeable status summaries.

### Spatial Rhythm
Padding inside operational cards uses `space-lg` (20px) on standard viewports and reduces to `space-md` (12px) in high-density audit grids. Spacing tokens never dictate fixed widths or heights—they govern padding and element gaps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on crisp, architectural separation rather than dramatized lighting effects. Surfaces separate via razor-thin lines, tonal backdrops, and low-amplitude, neutral drop shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Pure Slate-50 base `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation.
- **Layer 1 (Modular Work Surface):** Pure White `#FFFFFF` card containers bordered with a 1px solid stroke in `#E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0px 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Floating & Flyouts):** Dropdowns, popovers, quick-filter trays, and active date-range selectors. Raised using a refined enterprise drop shadow: `0px 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0px 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)` paired with a 1px `#E2E8F0` border.
- **Layer 3 (Modal Execution & Tactical Drawer):** Order fulfillment sheets, export confirmation overlays, and RTS review panels. Shadow: `0px 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0px 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` over an overlay backdrop of `#0F172A` with 40% opacity.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a structured, low-curvature visual language that maximizes informational real estate while softening terminal interfaces.

### Curvature Guidelines
- **Core Structural Units (Inputs, Cards, Panels, Modals):** Base curvature applies `0.375rem` (6px) to `0.5rem` (8px), enforcing crisp edges and enterprise discipline.
- **Status Telemetry & Micro-Pills:** Badges, operational queues, and offline synchronization tags use a 9999px full-pill radius. This distinct contrast immediately separates static interactive cards from dynamic live-state indicators.
- **Action Elements:** Primary and secondary buttons match card corner radii at 6px, reinforcing precise linear alignment across forms and toolbars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Deep Navy (`#0F172A`) background, White (`#FFFFFF`) label, subtle hover transition to Slate 800 (`#1E293B`). Micro-radius: 6px. Padding: 8px 16px. Height: 36px (compact density).
- **Secondary Action:** Pure White background, 1px solid border (`#E2E8F0`), text in Slate 900 (`#0F172A`). Hover transitions to Slate 50 (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Tactical Utility (Export / Sync):** Outlined button with Emerald accent highlight (`#10B981`) on active sync and an integrated circular status indicator.
- **Danger (Order Void, Return Reject):** Soft red background (`#FEF2F2`), border in `#FCA5A5`, text in Crimson Red (`#EF4444`). Active hover triggers solid `#EF4444` with white text.

### Badges & Status Pills
- Pill-shaped (9999px radius), low-saturation background with high-contrast text and a left-aligned 6px status dot:
  - **Net Profit / Completed:** `#ECFDF5` background, `#065F46` text, `#10B981` dot.
  - **Offline Sync Queue / Pending Review:** `#FFFBEB` background, `#92400E` text, `#F59E0B` dot (pulsing when syncing).
  - **Platform Deduction / Rejected Return:** `#FEF2F2` background, `#991B1B` text, `#EF4444` dot.

### Inputs & Search Bars
- Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, interior text Slate 900 (`#0F172A`), placeholder text Slate 400 (`#94A3B8`).
- Focus state: Subtle 1px ring in Deep Navy (`#0F172A`) paired with `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #0F172A`. Height: 36px for dense grids; keyboard-shortcut badge (`⌘K`) anchored to the right edge.

### Data Tables & Lists
- Table header: `#F8FAFC` background, uppercase `label-sm` typography in Slate 500 (`#64748B`), bottom border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`.
- Cells: Vertical padding 10px, horizontal padding 14px, bottom border 1px solid `#F1F5F9`. Numeric values right-aligned with `tnum` tabular lining.
- Interactive states: Row hover shifts to `#F8FAFC`. Selected items highlight in soft Slate-100 tint.

### Cards & Analytical Panels
- White `#FFFFFF` container with a 1px border in `#E2E8F0`, rounded to 8px.
- Metric cards feature a structured three-row anatomy: Category Label (Slate 500), Main Metric Display (`metric-display` in Slate 900), and Metric Variance Footer displaying trend pills (Emerald or Crimson).

### Offline Sync Telemetry Indicator
- Persistent utility status component anchored within the top command bar.
- Shows a dual-state badge: Solid Green for "Live Cloud Sync", pulsing Amber for "Offline Queue Active (N Pending)", accompanied by a one-click manual force-flush trigger.
